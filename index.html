How it works

Three steps from raw log to resolved incident.

01Detect

Wazuh SIEM ingests logs and flags suspicious activity — brute force, webshell uploads.

02Investigate

The AI agent correlates events, enriches with VirusTotal, maps to MITRE ATT&CK.

03Approve & Act

Reversible actions auto-execute. Irreversible ones wait for an engineer's approval.
